CAPACITY FORMULA
======================================================================

  Capacity = Available Slots (Calendly) + Booked Calls (Close CRM)

  Example: If Calendly shows 35 available and Close shows 20 booked:
    Capacity = 35 + 20 = 55
    Utilization = 20 / 55 = 36.4%
    Available = 35

  vs current static approach:
    Capacity = 42 (hardcoded)
    Utilization = 20 / 42 = 47.6%
    Available = 42 - 20 = 22

  The Calendly number is more accurate because it accounts for:
  - Reps with time off / blocked calendars
  - Days with fewer/more slots than average
  - Weekends and holidays automatically
